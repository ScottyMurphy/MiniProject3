#Validation Reflection - Smurph61

I validated ten projects with 5 fields totaling 50 fields. I found 26 areas of disagreement before talking with my partner. Our primary areas of disagreement included HasRecovered, WhyRecovered, and RecentThemes. We had a complete agreement on ActivityPattern classifications. Mostly, I would disagree with my partner on the definition of whether a given project has recovered from a state of inactivity. After discussing we determined that a project recovered if it had a substantial amount of commits after the project’s largest gap with no significant changes to its level of activity.

There were several patterns on both sides that contributed to our disagreement. Firstly, for RecentThemes, my partner only listed one responce for each field, casuing me to have to disagree with all of her values. We also had differences in defining CurrentStatus. We were able to agree on using April 1, 2025, as a cutoff point for determining whether the repository was active at the time of validation. We found this date in the MiniProject README.

When reviewing areas of disagreement, I was able to identify specific evidence that my partner could use to revise her results and conclusions. Our biggest disagreements stemmed from differences in definition. As such, I think that my recommendations and revisions were critical in standardizing our results and increasing the level of accuracy of our analysis. My largest takeaway from the validation was the importance of definitions and standards. This helps avoid subjectivity and strengthens the analysis to allow for factual evidence.
